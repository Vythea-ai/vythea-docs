The mobile app is Vythea on your phone, for iPhone and Android. You sign in with the same account as vythea.com, and your credit is the same.

> **Not in the stores yet.** The app is not on the App Store or Google Play yet. This guide describes it as it will ship.

The phone is a client. Answers come from Vythea's [hosted models](../hosted-models.md), from your own computer, or from Vythea Cloud. On an iPhone, chat can also answer with no internet, from a model on the phone.

## Chat and Code

The app has two sides. Open the chats with a swipe to the right anywhere on the chat, then choose **Chat** or **Code** at the top.

- **Chat** is for talking to a model. Each chat runs in the cloud or on your computer.
- **Code** is for sessions on a GitHub repo. Each message runs in Vythea Cloud and opens a pull request.

## Languages

The app is written in English and Japanese. It follows your phone's language, or the one you pick in Settings > Language.
