---

Open the chats, then **Settings** at the foot of the list.

| Group | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Account** | your **Credit**, and what you **Spent, 30 days** with the number of asks |
| **Computers** | your computers, and **Pair with a computer**; see [Your computer](your-computer.md) |
| **GitHub** | the link for [code sessions](code-sessions.md) |
| **Signed in on** | the phones and browsers your account is on; see [Signing in](signing-in.md) |
| **Appearance** | **System**, **Night** or **Paper** |
| **Language** | **Phone**, English or 日本語 |
| **Web search** | **Auto**, **Free** or **Paid**; see [Web search and photos](search-and-photos.md) |
| **Voice input** | who transcribes what you say |
| **On this iPhone** | models on the phone; see [Working offline](offline.md) |

Credit is added on vythea.com, not in the app. Pull down on Settings to load it again.

## Voice input

| Choice | Does |
|---|---|
| **Vythea** | ElevenLabs Scribe on Vythea transcribes it; the price a minute shows here, from your credit |
| **On phone** | the phone transcribes it, for free; less accurate, and only in the app's language |

With **Vythea**, the phone still transcribes when Vythea cannot be reached, or the account has no credit. **Model** picks which transcription model to use.

## Language

The app follows your phone's language unless you pick one. The keyboard and system sheets always follow the phone.
